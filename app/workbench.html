          Sending samples to the sampler by MIDI

          Uses the MIDI Sample Dump Standard, the same method WaveLab used. Needs Chrome or Edge. It is slow, about 2 KB per second, so it suits short samples. Not yet tested on a real S3000XL.

          Cables

          1. Connect your MIDI interface OUT to the S3000XL MIDI IN.
          2. Connect the S3000XL MIDI OUT back to your interface IN. This return cable lets the app read the sampler's list to find and name the new sample.

          In this app

          1. On the Samples tab, select the sample to send. Start with a short one.
          2. Open the MIDI send tab and press Connect MIDI. Allow the browser's MIDI permission when asked.
          3. Choose your interface as MIDI output and as MIDI input.
          4. Under What to send, choose "Selected sample" or "All loaded samples".
          5. Leave Handshaking on "Off (timed, no replies)".
          6. Press Send. The progress bar and percentage run to 100%, and the log shows what happens.

          Afterwards

          1. Press the SAMPLE button on the sampler.
          2. Turn the data knob to find your new sample.

          Auto sampling a synth

          Works from Chrome or Edge. If the meters stay still, the page lists the usual causes under the meters after a few seconds.

          Recording through ASIO. Browsers cannot open ASIO drivers, so the workbench comes with a small helper program. Put asio_helper.py, Install ASIO helper.bat and Start ASIO helper.bat in one folder. Run the install file once, then before each session double-click Start ASIO helper.bat and leave its window open. On the Auto sample tab set Source to ASIO helper, choose the device and inputs, and press Enable input. Close any DAW first: ASIO lets one program use the card at a time. Monitor through the card itself (PatchMix or your interface's mixer).

          1. Connect the synth's audio outputs to your audio interface, and your MIDI interface OUT to the synth's MIDI IN.
          2. Open the Auto sample tab. Press Enable input and Connect MIDI, then choose the interface, MIDI output and channel.
          3. Press Test note and set the synth's volume so the meters peak around −6 dBFS and never turn red.
          4. Set the octave range and step (3 semitones gives one keygroup per 3 keys), the hold time and the name prefix. The line under the panels shows every note and whether it fits in the sampler's memory.
          5. Press Start auto sample. Each note is recorded, trimmed and looped. When the run ends, the samples appear on the Samples tab and a program on the Programs tab, with stereo samples split into -L (zone 1, pan L50) and -R (zone 2, pan R50).
          6. Save the files or build a disk image from the Export tab, or send the samples from the MIDI send tab.

          Saving samples on the S3000XL

          Do this on the sampler once your samples are loaded and assigned, so everything is backed up to your drive.

          1. Press SINGLE, then EDIT.
          2. Rename the program with the NAME button.
          3. Press F2 (KGRP) to open Keygroups.
          4. Press F6 (SAMPLE) to assign your sample to the layout.
          5. Press SAVE and choose ALL PROGRAMS + SAMPLES to back up everything safely to your drive. Make sure you select the right location where to save the samples.

          Auto sample

          Plays each note on your synth over MIDI, records it from your audio interface, then adds the samples and a ready-made program to the workbench. Record through Chrome's audio input, or through your sound card's ASIO driver with the ASIO helper (see Help).

          Audio input

          Needs the ASIO helper running on this PC: double-click Start ASIO helper.bat and leave its window open. On an E-MU card, route the synth's input strip to a HOST ASIO send in PatchMix; those are the inputs listed here.

          MIDI and notes

          Octaves use the workbench's naming, middle C (60) = C3.

          Timing

          Each note is held for Hold, then recording stops once the release stays below the silence level for 150 ms, or after Max release tail. Silence before and after the note is trimmed and the end gets a 20 ms fade.

          SoundHold sTail sGap sdBFS

          With Auto-loop off, Hold is the longest a note can sustain on the sampler. For long pads, turn on Auto-loop or record mono to save memory.
